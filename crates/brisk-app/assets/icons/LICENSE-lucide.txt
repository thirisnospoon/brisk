Where the icons in this folder come from:

- Brisk's own, drawn for this project and covered by Brisk's license (MIT OR Apache-2.0):

  island-* (panel corners), dot, play-solid, stop-solid.

  logos, used only to mark files of those types. "Python" and the Python logos are trademarks

- Modified Lucide icons: check-bold (check with a heavier stroke) and sort-failed (built from
